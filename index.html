Instructions

  • Place 4 dots in the given Cartesian plane
  • Both players must place 4 dots before the game starts
  • When both players have placed their 4 dots, a countdown will start
  • Once the countdown hits 0, the game will begin
  • Input a slope formula in the format: y = mx + b
  • You have 20 seconds to input a formula
  • A red dot means you hit a player's dot
  • A yellow dot means your dot was hit
  • The game continues until one player hits all opponent dots
  • Players may choose a rematch after the game

    About Battle-Slopes

    Battle-Slopes is a competitive two-player math strategy game inspired by Battleship. Players place ships on a Cartesian plane and attack using linear equations.

    Victory requires precision, slope mastery, and strategic thinking. Every equation matters.
